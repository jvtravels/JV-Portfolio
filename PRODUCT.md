# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Two audiences, weighted equally: (1) startup founders and small teams evaluating Jay Vyas for freelance/contract design work, and (2) hiring managers/recruiters at companies evaluating him for a full-time senior product designer role. Both arrive to judge craft quickly, scan case studies and testimonials, and decide whether to reach out or move on.

## Product Purpose

A personal design portfolio for Jay Vyas, Senior Product Designer. The site itself is the product: it exists to demonstrate design craft directly (not just describe it), showcase shipped work (Tempo, EVES, DynaChrg PAYG, Cypher, Specter, The Signal, Calldesk, Cluster), build trust through brand logos and testimonials, and convert visitors into an inbound project inquiry or hiring conversation via the "Start a Project" CTA.

## Brand Personality

Confident, precise, minimal. Understated dark editorial feel: the work speaks for itself, ornamentation stays low, craft stays tight. Voice is direct and grounded, not hypey.

## Anti-references

Explicitly not a generic SaaS/AI-startup template: no hero-metric blocks (big number + small label + gradient accent), no gradient text, no identical feature-card grids, no stock "AI abstract gradient" imagery. The site should read as hand-crafted and specific to one designer, not templated.

## Design Principles

- Show, don't tell: let real shipped work and metrics-free case studies carry the argument, not adjectives.
- One consistent framing device: the dashed-line section frame is the site's signature structural motif and must stay geometrically flush and rhythmically consistent everywhere it appears.
- Restraint over noise: dark, low-chroma neutral base with a single warm accent (orange CTA), used sparingly and consistently.
- Editorial pacing: generous vertical rhythm and scroll-reveal motion should feel calm and confident, never flashy or bouncy.
- Serve both audiences from the same content: nothing should read as freelance-only or corporate-only.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No explicit WCAG target set by the user; default to WCAG AA as a baseline (color contrast, focus states, alt text, keyboard navigability) unless told otherwise.
